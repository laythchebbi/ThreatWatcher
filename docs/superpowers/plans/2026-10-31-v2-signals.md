# Threat Watcher v2 — Plan 2 of 3: Signals (certificates, pages, RDAP, posture, leak mentions)

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The watch cycle sees a campaign earlier and more precisely: certificates before DNS, live pages with a credential form, who registered the lookalike and when, the customer's own email posture, and leak mentions from crawled dark-web pages and Have I Been Pwned.

**Architecture:** Five new pure watch steps (`certs`, `http`, `rdap`, `posture`, `leaks`) plug into `runCycle` beside the Plan 1 steps, each taking injected `fetchImpl`/`resolver` and returning plain enrichments; the diff grows new event kinds; external providers sit behind one in-process token bucket and a per-label cache. Leak mentions get their own collection and page. The Tor crawler is ported unchanged except for dropping `KEYWORDS`.

**Tech Stack:** as Plan 1 (Node 20, Express 4, EJS, mongoose 8, zod 4, node:test, mongodb-memory-server) plus `node:tls` for certificate fingerprints and `cheerio` 1.0.0 (one new dependency, already vetted in the audit) for HTML parsing. Python 3.9 crawler with `requests`, `beautifulsoup4`, `pymongo`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-30-threat-watcher-v2-design.md` sections 7.3 (leak mentions, cert and page rules, severity), 7.4 steps 2, 3b, 4, 5a, 5b, 7.6 (`/dark-web`, `/domains/:id` additions), plus `2026-10-31-intelligence-roadmap.md` section 5 items 1–2 (fields stored on the HTTP step so Plan 4 can read them). Plan 1 (`2026-10-31-v2-core.md`) is merged; its interfaces are consumed here.

## Global Constraints

- Everything in Plan 1's Global Constraints still applies (CommonJS, gates green after every task, exact-pinned deps, port 3100 for local boots, pure steps with injected deps).
- External calls: `fetchImpl` default `fetch`, every call with `AbortSignal.timeout(ms)`: crt.sh 20 000, Cert Spotter 15 000, HTTP page 8 000, RDAP 10 000, HIBP 15 000. One token bucket per provider shared by all cycles: crt.sh 30/min, Cert Spotter 60/min, dnstwister 30/min, RDAP 60/min, HIBP 10/min.
- Certificate queries skip labels shorter than 5 characters. Results cached per label for 6 h. Cert Spotter is primary with a per-label `after` cursor; crt.sh is the first backfill and the fallback. Body caps: crt.sh 5 MB and 5 000 rows.
- HTTP step: `GET https://` then `http://`, 8 s, ≤ 3 redirects, fixed `User-Agent: ThreatWatcher/2 (+https://github.com/...)`, decoded body cap 256 KB, at most 8 in flight per cycle, first cycle capped to the 50 highest-similarity resolving sightings. SSRF guard: resolve the host first and refuse loopback, private (RFC 1918), link-local, ULA, multicast, and `169.254.169.254`; repeat after every redirect.
- `page_mimics_login` = `<input type=password>` present **and** a brand term (label or `terms[]`) in title, visible text or link hosts. Text similarity against `https://<domain>/` is stored, never used for the event.
- Severity (adds to Plan 1): `cert_issued` medium, high if the sighting also resolves or is punycode/substring; `page_live` high; `page_mimics_login` critical; `posture_weak` low; `leak_mention` medium for an auto-derived label match, high for a customer term or the full domain, critical only when the excerpt holds an `@<domain>` address or the full domain name, and password/combo/dump within 200 characters bumps one level at most.
- Baseline (adds to Plan 1): certificates with `notBefore` before `firstSeenAt` never raise `cert_issued`; `cert_issued` fires once per sighting; on `baseline: true` sightings only `cert_issued` (post-baseline) and `page_mimics_login` are un-tagged.
- RDAP: names registered more than a year ago with no change stay low and silent; a sighting whose registrar and nameservers match the customer's own domain is auto-set `state: "ignored"` with `rdap.defensive: true`.
- Terms: 4–40 characters, at most 5 per domain, rejected when they match more than 5 % of crawled pages. Matching is case-insensitive, whole-word, `-` and space alike.
- HIBP: the customer's own key, stored on the domain; `User-Agent` names the product; the domain page credits HIBP (CC BY 4.0); poll `/latestBreach` once per cycle and `/breachedDomain/<domain>` only when it changed.

## Review Focus

1. A redirect from a public host to `http://127.0.0.1/` or `http://169.254.169.254/` must be refused mid-chain, not followed; test in Task 3 (`refuses private targets after a redirect`).
2. A Let's Encrypt renewal (new `tbs_sha256`, same name, 60 days later) must update `cert.lastSeenAt` and raise nothing; test in Task 6 (`cert renewal is silent`).
3. A label of four characters (`acme`) must skip certificate queries entirely, including the cache and the bucket; test in Task 2 (`short labels skip CT`).
4. A term that is a substring of a common word (`acme` inside `acmes`) must not match whole-word, and a hyphenated brand must match its spaced form; test in Task 8 (`term matching is whole-word and hyphen/space tolerant`).
5. A dismissed mention whose page is re-crawled with the same excerpt must stay silent; a changed excerpt re-raises; test in Task 8 (`dismissed mention re-raises only on excerpt change`).
